Over-the-air (OTA) are channels that you can pick up with just an antenna. [TV Fool](http://www.tvfool.com/) will show you which channels you can receive at your house with an antenna and the measures needed for them to come in clearly. I remember trying to play with our antenna in the 1980's hoping that I could clear up the signal and get rid of the static, digital channels don't have static. They are either clear or unavailable.
