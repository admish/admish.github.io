I've installed a collection of programs on a dedicated device at home to create my own media server. The majority of them are free, but require access to a paid service to download files.

Everything is focused around the streaming service Plex. Plex has a free option, but for a small yearly fee of about $40, or $300 for a lifetime pass, they offer additional features. I also have a domain name to give everything a memorable URL and enable HTTPS on those URL. Overall I spend $171.75 a year plus the cost of the hardware.

Apps:

* Sonarr - TV Shows
* Couchpotato - Movies
* Headphones - Music
* LazyLibrarian - Books
* Mylar - Comic Books

Pricing:

* Plex $39.99/yr
* Domain $35.76 /yr
* Usenet $8/mo
* NZB Indexer $30 /Lifetime
